Our story

Learning that strengthens both knowledge and character.

Imamu Qaloon Academy is an Islamic and digital education center located in Yelwa, Plateau State, Nigeria. Our mission is to provide authentic Qur'an education, Arabic language learning, Islamic studies, and digital skills in a way that is both spiritually rooted and practically useful.

Our mission

To provide authentic Qur'an education, Arabic learning, Islamic studies, and digital skills.

We are committed to making Islamic learning accessible and meaningful through structured study, sincere guidance, and a strong connection to real-life practice.

Our approach

To serve learners through both physical and online classes.

Whether students learn on-site or through remote instruction, our goal is to create a supportive environment where learning can thrive.

Authentic teaching

Our study is rooted in the correct understanding of the Qur'an, Sunnah-informed guidance, and sound Islamic knowledge.

Faith-centered focus

We help learners grow in knowledge, character, and daily practice through balanced Islamic education.

Progress with purpose

Each learner is guided with patience and structure, whether studying Tajweed, memorization, Arabic, or digital literacy.

Inclusive and welcoming

Children, youth, and adults are all welcome, with flexible learning paths available both in-person and online.
