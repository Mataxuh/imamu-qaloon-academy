Admissions

Begin with clarity and confidence.

Choosing the right Islamic learning path starts with understanding your goals, current level, and the kind of support you need. At Imamu Qaloon Academy, you can learn through physical classes or 100% online programs.

1. Discover your goals

Tell us whether you are seeking Qur'an memorization, Tajweed, Arabic studies, Islamic studies, or digital literacy support.

2. Share your starting point

We will learn more about your current level, preferred schedule, and whether you prefer online or physical classes.

3. Choose a path

Once the right fit is identified, we can recommend a practical program pathway that supports steady progress.

4. Start with intention

Every learner begins at a meaningful place. Our aim is consistent progress rooted in sincerity, discipline, and guidance.

Guidance

Questions we often hear.

These are common questions families ask before beginning their learning journey with Imamu Qaloon Academy (IQA).

Ask a question
Where should a complete beginner start?

Start with the subject that matters most to your goals. For Qur'an reading, Tajweed, Arabic, or Islamic studies, foundational learning is a natural first step.

Can I explore programs for my child?

Yes. Parents and guardians may request a consultation to discuss age appropriateness, learning style, and the best program fit.

Do you offer flexible learning options?

Yes. The academy provides both physical classes and 100% online learning options to suit different schedules and circumstances.
