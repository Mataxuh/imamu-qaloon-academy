Cookies policy

This cookies policy explains how Imamu Qaloon Academy (IQA) uses cookies and similar technologies on our website.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files saved on your device when you visit a website. They help us provide a smoother browsing experience and understand how visitors use the site.

Why we use cookies

  • To remember your preferences and improve website usability
  • To understand which pages are most useful to visitors
  • To support secure and efficient site operations
  • To help us improve our courses, admissions process, and communication experience

Types of cookies we may use

We may use essential cookies that are necessary for the website to function correctly, as well as analytics or preference cookies to help us improve the service.

Managing cookies

You can control or disable cookies from your browser settings at any time. Please note that disabling some cookies may affect the functionality of certain parts of the website.

Updates to this policy

We may revise this cookies policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with the updated date.
